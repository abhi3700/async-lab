**Status:** Planned

## Outcome

Turn invisible task state into evidence and make timing-sensitive behavior reproducible.

## Build

Instrument a small service, inspect its tasks and resources, test time-dependent behavior with paused
time, and model a synchronization boundary under multiple interleavings.

## Evidence of completion

Diagnose an intermittent stall from structured observations instead of adding arbitrary delays.
