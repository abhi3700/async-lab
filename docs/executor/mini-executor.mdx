**Status:** Planned

## Outcome

Build an executor that exposes the relationship between a future, task, waker, and ready queue.

## Build

Implement `Task`, `Spawner`, and `Executor`, then run multiple futures whose wake-ups place their tasks
back on the queue.

## Evidence of completion

Explain every ownership edge and show why pending tasks sleep until a wake-up makes them runnable.
