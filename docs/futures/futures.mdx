**Status:** Planned

## Outcome

Understand a future as a value that must be polled to make progress.

## Build

Implement and manually drive a countdown future that returns `Pending` a known number of times before
returning `Ready`. Contrast useful notification-driven polling with a busy loop.

## Evidence of completion

Explain who calls `poll`, why futures are lazy, what `.await` does at a suspension point, and why
`Pending` alone is insufficient for efficient progress.
