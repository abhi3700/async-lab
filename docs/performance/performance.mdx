**Status:** Planned

## Outcome

Measure async behavior across workload scales and explain the result with runtime mechanics.

## Measurements

- memory per task and allocation count;
- scheduling and wake-up latency;
- channel throughput and mutex contention;
- syscalls, batching, cache locality, false sharing, and atomics;
- topology and NUMA effects where the environment permits.

## Evidence of completion

Provide a reproducible benchmark, state its environmental limits, and distinguish observations from
inferences.
