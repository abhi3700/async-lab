**Status:** Planned

## Outcome

Compose task lifecycles while understanding their ownership and scheduling constraints.

## Topics

`spawn`, `JoinHandle`, `JoinSet`, `join!`, `try_join!`, `select!`, unordered futures, `yield_now`,
`spawn_local`, and `LocalSet`.

## Evidence of completion

Diagnose `Send`, `'static`, and `!Send` failures and explain whether work is concurrent, parallel, or
merely interleaved.
