**Status:** Planned

## Outcome

Keep producers, consumers, and shutdown paths within explicit resource bounds.

## Build

Combine bounded channels, a semaphore, timeouts, selection, and a cancellation token in a small
pipeline with graceful shutdown.

## Evidence of completion

Explain what is dropped, what cleanup still runs, how in-flight work terminates, and what limits queue
growth when the producer is faster than the consumer.
