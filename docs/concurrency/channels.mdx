## Outcome

Select a channel by its information-flow contract rather than by familiarity.

## Build

Compare `mpsc`, `oneshot`, `broadcast`, and `watch`, then build a request/response actor that owns a
resource behind asynchronous operations.

## Evidence of completion

Explain senders, receivers, capacity, lag, closure, cancellation, and response routing for the chosen
design.
